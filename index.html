Paste without Line Breaks

The app lives in the menu bar (¶ icon). Select text anywhere and press ⌃⌘C (Control + Command + C) instead of ⌘C. The text is copied without styles, line breaks and end-of-line hyphens, ready to paste into a translator. A normal ⌘C keeps working as usual.

The first time you use the shortcut, macOS asks you to allow Paste without Line Breaks under System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility. The app needs it only to press ⌘C for you.

Menu bar options

  • Remove All Line Breaks – everything becomes one line.
  • Keep Paragraphs – wrapped lines are joined, empty lines between paragraphs stay.
  • Only Remove Styles – plain text, line breaks untouched.
  • Also Clean Every ⌘C Copy – the old behaviour: every copy is cleaned automatically.
